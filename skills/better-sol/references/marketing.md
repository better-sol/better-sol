# Marketing and Video

## Launch message

Use this structure:

- Who it is for
- Pain it removes
- What changed because of the product
- Proof that it works
- Clear next action

## Video formats

- Hackathon demo: proof-heavy, 2–3 minutes.
- Product promo: benefit-heavy, 30–60 seconds.
- Technical walkthrough: architecture-heavy, 3–6 minutes.
- Social clip: one idea, 10–30 seconds.

## Storyboard template

```markdown
## Scene [number]
Goal:
Visual:
Caption:
Voiceover:
Proof shown:
Transition:
```

## Avoid

- generic “revolutionizing Web3” claims
- unexplained token jargon
- fake metrics
- product screenshots too small to read
- demos that hide signing/confirmation
