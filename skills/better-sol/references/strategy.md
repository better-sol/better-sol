# Strategy, Validation, and Landscape

## Idea scoring rubric

Score 1–5:

- painful problem
- clear user and buyer
- Solana necessity
- Better Sol fit
- buildability in available time
- distribution access
- differentiation
- demo quality
- trust path

A hackathon idea needs buildability and demo quality. A startup idea needs pain and distribution. A grant idea needs ecosystem benefit and credible deliverables.

## Interview prompts

- Who has this problem today?
- What do they do instead?
- Why does this need Solana rather than a database?
- What must be on-chain?
- What can be integrated instead of built?
- What proof would convince a skeptic?
- What can be validated in 48 hours?

## Validation sprint

```markdown
## Hypothesis
[belief being tested]

## Segment
[target users]

## Test
[interviews / landing page / prototype / outbound / waitlist / paid test]

## Pass threshold
[number or qualitative signal]

## Fail threshold
[what kills or pivots the idea]

## Next action
[if pass / if fail]
```

## Competitive landscape

Map:

- direct competitors
- substitutes
- protocols to integrate
- user workarounds
- distribution channels
- trust advantages
- technical wedges
- gaps

## Positioning formula

```text
For [specific user] who [pain], [product] is a [category] that [benefit]. Unlike [alternative], it [differentiator/proof].
```
