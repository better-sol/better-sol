# Brand System

## Brand interview

Ask for:

- user segment
- category
- desired emotion
- competitors to avoid resembling
- conservative vs experimental direction
- dark/light requirements

## Palette requirements

A crypto product palette needs:

- background
- elevated surface
- primary text
- muted text
- primary action
- success
- warning
- danger
- border
- focus ring
- chart colors if dashboard exists

## Directions

### Stark protocol

Black/white, thin borders, high contrast, precise copy, low motion. Good for developer tools and serious protocols.

### Warm trust

Charcoal, sand, amber, soft panels, human copy. Good for consumer finance and onboarding.

### Trading terminal

Dark base, dense layout, green/red deltas, mono numerals. Good for analytics, DeFi, perps.

### Soft wallet

Light surfaces, rounded controls, friendly accent, plain language. Good for consumer flows.
